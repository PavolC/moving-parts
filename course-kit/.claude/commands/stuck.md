I stopped here: $ARGUMENTS

This is a comprehension bug report, not a style note. Treat my words as the evidence.

1. **Do not defend the passage.** Find the mechanism of the failure, not a nicer wording.
   Reread the passage against the playbook and name which rule it broke, or say honestly
   that no existing rule covers it.
2. Ask me at most two questions if you genuinely cannot tell what I misread. Otherwise
   just work.
3. **Fix it structurally.** The fixes that worked in course one were reorderings, figures
   and worked examples, not adjectives: move the interactive earlier, log the numbers
   first, draw the thing, pick an example where the mechanism is visible, name the pattern
   and sort the instances into kinds.
4. **Then generalize, in the same commit:**
   - a rule in `CLAUDE.md`, imperative, one or two sentences, in my words where they are
     better than yours;
   - the incident in `CASEBOOK.md`: my quote, what was actually wrong, the fix, the cost;
   - a `[casebook: N]` pointer from the rule to the incident.
5. **Then sweep.** The same bug is almost never in one place. Check every other chapter for
   it and say what you found, even if you do not fix it all now.

A fix that does not leave a rule behind will be re-learned.
