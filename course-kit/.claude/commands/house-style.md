---
description: Measure every chapter against the ones that worked, on countable prose features.
---

Scope: $ARGUMENTS (default: every chapter)

This is an audit, not a rewrite. Measure first, then fix only what the numbers flag.

1. **Write or run the script** that computes, per chapter: sentence count, median sentence
   length, cleft and abstract-first openers as a share of sentences, paragraphs opening with
   a pronoun, parentheticals per paragraph, callbacks per paragraph, and em dashes (which
   must be zero). Commit the script, so the bands in `CLAUDE.md` are checkable rather than
   aspirational.
2. **Report the table**, with the bands from `CLAUDE.md` beside each column, and mark every
   chapter that falls outside one.
3. **Read the outliers** and diagnose them. Remember the counterintuitive one: prose that
   reads badly is usually not too long. Cutting words is what produces telegraphese;
   rejoin clauses instead.
4. Apply the register test to every flagged passage: could this sentence appear unchanged in
   a careful colleague's explanation email?
5. Fix, and re-run the script. Show me the before and after table.
